# Brand asset provenance

- **Logo**: from the restaurant's brand guide (Canva export, 2026-10-21; originals
  in the gitignored `data/brand/`), traced to vector in `src/components/brand/logo.tsx`.
- **panda-7.webp**: Panda 7, the official mascot from November 2026 (holding a
  bowl, thumbs-up). Cut from the client's artwork (`data/photos-nov/`) with a
  transparent background. It replaces the brand guide's 3D poses.
